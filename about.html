Professional Summary

Dr. Ing. Costel-Ciprian Raicu is active as an Associate Professor within the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Department of Measurements, Electrical Apparatus and Static Control (DMAECS) at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

His research focuses on automotive lighting systems, 48V vehicle networks, DC/DC drives, sensors, and precise electrical measurement apparatus.
